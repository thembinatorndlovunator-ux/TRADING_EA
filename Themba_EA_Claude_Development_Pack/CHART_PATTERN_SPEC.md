# TECHNICAL CHART-PATTERN SPECIFICATION

Initial supported patterns:

- Double top and bottom.
- Triple top and bottom.
- Head and shoulders and inverse.
- Ascending, descending, and symmetrical triangles.
- Rectangle.
- Bull and bear flags.
- Pennants.
- Rising and falling wedges.
- Ascending and descending channels.

For every pattern define:

- Required confirmed pivots.
- Price and time tolerances.
- Minimum ATR height.
- Minimum and maximum duration.
- Trend prerequisite.
- Boundary or neckline.
- Breakout rule.
- Retest rule.
- Target.
- Stop.
- Invalidation.
- Expiration.
- Confidence.
- Eligible regime.
- Eligible intraday mode.

Statuses:

- FORMING
- CONFIRMED
- RETESTING
- TRADED
- INVALIDATED
- EXPIRED

Do not trade FORMING patterns.
Do not reposition confirmed historical pivots.
Draw pattern name, boundaries, neckline, breakout, retest, target, and invalidation.
